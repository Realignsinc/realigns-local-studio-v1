# Responsible AI Guidelines

## Realigns Local Studio v1

Realigns Local Studio v1 is designed to support responsible, lawful, and human-supervised AI use.

## Intended Use

The product is intended for:

- Business productivity
- Document assistance
- Internal research
- Drafting and summarization
- Coding support
- Presentation preparation
- Knowledge assistance
- Private local AI experimentation
- Controlled organizational AI workflows

## Restricted Use

Users must not use Realigns Local Studio v1 for:

- Illegal activity
- Fraud
- Malware or cyber abuse
- Harassment or abuse
- Harmful deception
- Unauthorized surveillance
- Privacy violations
- Unsafe medical, legal, or financial decisions without qualified professional review
- Any use that violates applicable law or organizational policy

## Human Oversight

AI outputs should be reviewed by a qualified human before important business, legal, financial, medical, technical, or operational decisions are made.

## Governance Alignment

Realigns Local Studio v1 is designed to support responsible AI governance principles, including:

- Privacy by design
- Human oversight
- Transparency
- Security awareness
- Controlled deployment
- Lawful use
- Risk-aware AI adoption

Compliance depends on each organization’s policies, deployment environment, model selection, and human review process.
